AM1 Digital Blade Angle Meter

Fast, accurate, and repeatable doctor blade angle measurements with dual-angle OLED display, 100-reading memory, and roll diameter compensation.

The AM1 Blade Angle Meter is specifically developed for easy and accurate doctor blade angle measurements on paper, board, and tissue machines. An on-line angle display together with an ultra-precise sensor and optimal digital signal filtering allows fast, accurate measurements in a single step without repetitions or averaging. Suitable for all doctor blade types and holders, including double doctor second blades.

Dual Front & Backside Angle Display 100-Reading Memory (No Hand Notes Needed) 0.1° High Resolution & Roll Diameter Compensation
Device Specifications

AM1 Properties & Performance

Equipped with a vivid OLED color display that shows both frontside and backside measurement values simultaneously. Features an internal memory of 100 readings (50 frontside, 50 backside) and customizable roll diameter compensation in 10mm increments through an intuitive menu.
