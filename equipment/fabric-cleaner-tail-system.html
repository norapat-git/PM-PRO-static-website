Fabric Cleaner, Tail Threading & Turn-Up

Next-generation EZ Tail Threading, EZ Jet High-Pressure Tail Cutter, EZ Eco Cleaner, and EZ Turn-Up systems to maximize efficiency and mill sustainability.

PMPRO in cooperation with APT-Daesung Engineering delivers state-of-the-art machine optimization systems to pulp, paper, and tissue mills. Engineered to maximize machine runnability, eliminate hands-on operator hazards, reduce water and energy consumption, and extend forming fabric and press felt lifespan.

Tail Threading Automation

EZ Tail Threading System

Ropeless threading system, vacuum belts (Size Press), and air threading pans. Delivers complete "hands-off" operator safety, shortest threading times, high repeatability from press to reel sections, and minimal compressed air energy consumption.

Ropeless & Vacuum Belt Hands-Off Operator Safety Press to Reel Section
High-Pressure Cutting

EZ Jet HP Tail Cutter

High-precision waterjet tail cutter featuring a Ø0.15mm sapphire diamond nozzle, 0.75kW induction motor with reduction gear, high-pressure pump up to 2,000 bar, 5-micron microfilter housing, and Siemens PLC automated control.

Sapphire Diamond 0.15mm Nozzle 2,000 Bar HP Pump System Siemens PLC Control
Continuous Fabric Cleaning

EZ Eco Fabric Cleaner

High-performance 700-900 RPM rotating cleaning head with 32 sapphire nozzles and 270 bar pump. Cleans forming fabrics and press felts continuously during full-speed production with zero chemicals and ultra-low water consumption, eliminating wash shutdowns and web breaks.

32 Sapphire Nozzles (700-900 RPM) Zero-Chemical Operation Extended Wire & Felt Lifespan
Reel Section Efficiency

EZ Jet Turn-Up System

Fully automatic high-pressure waterjet turn-up for reel sections. Achieves >99% turn-up efficiency, minimizes bottom paper loss down to 25m, guarantees operator safety, uses only 10ml of water per turn-up, and delivers a return on investment (ROI) in less than 3 months.

>99% Turn-Up Efficiency Bottom Loss <25m ROI Less Than 3 Months
