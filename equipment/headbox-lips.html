Headbox Top/Bottom Lips & Apron Boards

Premium-grade OEM replacement slice lips and aprons manufactured in the USA to restore exact cross-direction profile accuracy and paper formation.

Our manufacturing partner in the USA has been manufacturing top slice lips and apron boards for over 20 years, supplying critical lips to leading global paper machine manufacturers.
