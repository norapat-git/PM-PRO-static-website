Wintech Superfinishing Machines

Advanced WinHeavy roll grinder attachments, WinBeam in-machine finishing systems, and Barring Killer technology for paper mill rolls.

PMPRO in cooperation with Wintech delivers high-end superfinishing products engineered to drastically increase roll grinding efficiency. Wintech solutions eliminate costly diamond wheel regrinding on ceramic and tungsten carbide rolls, accelerate final grinding stages up to 80% faster than stones, and achieve mirror-finish roll surface Ra in-situ.

Up to 80% Faster Than Stone Grinding In-Machine Mirror Finishing with 1 Person Vibration-Free 200mm Wide Finishing Band
Heavy-Duty Grinder Attachment

Winheavy superfinishing machine

Engineered to increase roll grinding throughput and eliminate diamond wheel regrinding on hard ceramic center rolls and tungsten covered calender rolls. Rapidly removes grinding stone marks up to 80% faster. Vibration-free construction utilizes a 200mm wide finishing band with oscillation, and can be permanently installed onto existing roll grinder bedways.

Diamond Wheel Regrind Avoidance 200mm Wide Oscillating Band 80% Grinding Time Reduction
In-Situ Machine Finishing

Winbeam in-machine finishing

Achieve roll workshop surface smoothness directly inside the paper machine with minimal time and only one operator. Suitable for all roll types and covers. For example, a Ø600 x 5,000mm thermo roll is superfinished to a flawless mirror surface in just 2.5 hours total (including assembly and disassembly), with only 2 microns of material removed.

2.5 Hours Complete Mirror Finish Only 2 Microns Material Removed Single Operator Operation
Specialized Wintech Equipment

Barring Killer, Measurement Control & Specialty Tools

WinBeamBK (Barring Killer): Specifically designed to eliminate barring marks on polymer-covered calender rolls, extending roll change intervals and cover life by 2-3 times with only ~0.05mm removed per treatment.
WinBeamMC (Measurement Control): High-speed optical/electronic runout, profile, and pitch mark measurement system delivering 1 to 10 micron precision reports in minutes.
Additional Lineup: WinGroove, WinDress, Winlathe, and Winschleif specialized tooling.
