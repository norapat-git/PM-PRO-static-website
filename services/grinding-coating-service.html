Grinding and Coating Service

Precision on-site roll grinding, Tungsten Carbide thermal coating, Teflon repellent treatments, and metalizing in cooperation with Ralex Industry.

PMPRO is working in cooperation with Ralex Industry who has many years of global experience for specialized on-site services like grinding, coating, thermal spraying, and metalizing. We service dryer cylinders, Yankee dryers, reel drums, and rewinder drums in-situ without costly dismantling, reducing downtime while protecting the surrounding mill environment.

Precision Surface Grinding

On-site grinding service

A clean dryer cylinder with an accurate profile allows good and even heat transfer to the paper web, improving paper profiles. A reel drum with an accurate profile ensures uniform Jumbo reel density and minimized machine vibration. On-site grinding of dryer cylinders, Yankee dryers, and reel drums achieves exact diameter profiles and specified surface Ra requirements.

Dryer, Yankee & Reel Drums Exact Surface Ra Finish Uniform Heat Transfer Profile
Thermal Spray & Non-Stick

On-site coating service

On-site coating with Tungsten carbide applied to dryer cylinders and winder drums, with optional Teflon repellent coatings for anti-stick properties. We coat rewinder drums with Tungsten Carbide on-site without dismantling or polluting the mill environment, tailoring surface roughness precisely to paper grade specifications.

Tungsten Carbide Thermal Spray Teflon Repellent Layer Zero-Dismantling Application
Diameter Restoration & Repair

On-site metalizing service

When dryer cylinder diameter discrepancies occur across a drive group or when localized surface damage is present, metalizing is the ideal solution to build up roll diameters and restore damaged areas, ensuring synchronized web draw and prolonged equipment life.
