Nip Test and Tuning

Advanced hydraulic system diagnostics, multichannel pressure analysis, nip force calibration, and CD nip profile optimization.

Hydraulic systems represent a large proportion in machinery but still today, many problems relate to hydraulics and they are not often visible to operators, making it difficult to connect hydraulic problems as a root cause to runnability or profile issues. Fixing discovered anomalies allows maintenance teams to prioritize shutdown operations effectively. The nip loading test gives vital insight into the operational condition of hydraulic systems, delivering valuable data on moisture profiles, caliper variations, and premature roll cover or fabric wear. Possible causes of incorrect nip loading include pivot friction, cylinder seal resistance, faulty pressure transmitters, or inaccurate roll crown curves. PMPRO in cooperation with ProTest Engineering provides specialized testing and precision tuning tailored to your mill's exact needs.

Nip test and tuning service

The aim of the study is to evaluate the existing condition of the entire nip loading system. Scope includes shutdown pressure measurements for loading hydraulics and crown control roll hydraulics (including hydraulic pumps), precision nip force measurements, calibration of loading mechanics, and dynamic nip profile measurements. Applicable to press sections, size presses, and machine calenders.

Multichannel Pressure Analysis

Shutdown pressure measurement

Shutdown pressure measurements (multichannel pressure analysis) for loading and Crown Control Roll (CCR) hydraulic system components are vital to identify problems and prepare for tuning. All pressure measurements are captured in the time domain and transferred to the frequency domain to perform advanced root cause analysis of valve responses, pulsation, and pump stability.

Time & Frequency Domain Multichannel Data Acquisition CCR Hydraulic Valve Profiling
Linear Load Calibration

Nip force measurement

Nip force measurements are essential when uneven nip profiles or skewing occurs. Testing reveals actual linear load versus setpoint, differences between Tending Side (TS) and Drive Side (DS), mechanical cylinder friction, and mechanical servicing needs. Loading curves inside the machine control system are optimized to ensure even distribution and exact matching to operator setpoints.

Actual Load vs. Setpoint TS vs. DS Load Balancing Optimized Loading Curves
Fujifilm Nip Impression

Nip profile measurement

Nip profile measurements provide definitive data on crown control roll adjustable crowning functionality across the entire nip width using specialized Fujifilm pressure paper. Crown control roll internal pressure difference curves are optimized for the straightest possible cross-direction (CD) pressure profile, which is then updated directly into the PLC system.
